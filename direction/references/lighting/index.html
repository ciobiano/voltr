Automotive Studio Photography

Mercedes-Benz "The Silence"

Mercedes-Benz commercial (2019)

Technique

Soft key light at 45° + rim light tracing body lines + blacked-out background. 70% of frame is near-black.

Principle

Sculpt form with negative space. Show only what matters.

VOLTR application

Single soft key light from upper-left on hero product shots. Let 70% of frame fall to near-black. Rim light to outline UI surface edges.

Lexus "Gift" Commercial

Lexus brand film

Technique

Candlelit interior cross-fading to car's ambient cabin lighting. Warm fill + cold rim.

Principle

Warm fill light creates intimacy. Cold rim light = technology.

VOLTR application

Dual-temp lighting: 2700K accent for human elements (story, testimonials), 5600K cool white for data/UI panels.

BMW "The Electric" Spot

BMW brand film

Technique

Moving slit scan light passing over vehicle silhouette.

Principle

Linear light reveal builds anticipation.

VOLTR application

Animated gradient sweep across section titles — light passes left-to-right, revealing text in its wake.

Audi "Light is Everything"

Audi brand film

Technique

Single pinpoint spot moving across car in total darkness.

Principle

Theatrical isolation — what's lit is sacred.

VOLTR application

Spotlight cursor: mouse position reveals content beneath a dark overlay. Everything else stays hidden.

Volumetric Lighting in Film

Blade Runner 2049 (Deakins)

Cinematography by Roger Deakins

Technique

Hard beams through venetian blinds + floating atmospheric haze.

Principle

Light as a solid object. Air has mass and depth.

VOLTR application

box-shadow with blur(40px) on pseudo-elements for god-ray effects behind hero text. Layered radial-gradient for atmospheric depth between sections.

The Batman (Fraser)

Cinematography by Greig Fraser

Technique

Single red/gold beam cutting through smoke in vertical shafts.

Principle

Color-as-atmosphere defines emotional territory.

VOLTR application

Section background transitions use gradient fog layer (opacity 0→0.3→0) that scrolls at 0.5x speed — creates volumetric depth without WebGL.

Arrival (Bradford Young)

Cinematography by Bradford Young

Technique

Soft, dispersed light through semi-transparent ceiling panels.

Principle

Diffuse top-light = clarity, objectivity, calm.

VOLTR application

Top-down gradient on content cards: lightest at top edge, fading to background color — simulates overhead studio softbox.

Dune (Villeneuve / Fraser)

Cinematography by Greig Fraser

Technique

Atmospheric dust catching harsh desert sunlight.

Principle

Particle matter makes light visible.

VOLTR application

filter: blur() + mix-blend-mode: screen on repeating radial gradient to simulate dust particles catching light between sections.

Architectural Lighting Design

Tadao Ando — Church of the Light

Architecture by Tadao Ando

Technique

Single horizontal/vertical slit cut through concrete wall. Cross-shaped daylight enters through absence.

Principle

Light defined by absence. The cut matters more than the source.

VOLTR application

Section dividers are horizontal "slits" of bright color in an otherwise dark layout — like light through a concrete gap.

James Turrell — Ganzfeld Series

Light art installations

Technique

Whole-room immersion in single hue. Edges dissolve into uniform color. No reference points.

Principle

Perception is the medium. Remove reference points.

VOLTR application

Background color transitions (HSL shift) that happen so slowly the user doesn't notice until they scroll back — pure atmospheric mood.

Ando — Naoshima Museum

Architecture by Tadao Ando

Technique

Underground spaces lit only by reflected light from water pools.

Principle

Indirect light is softer, more contemplative.

VOLTR application

Cards use subtle box-shadow to suggest light bouncing up from below — ground-plane illumination.

Turrell — Roden Crater

Light art installation, Arizona

Technique

Aperture to sky frames celestial light. Color changes every moment with time of day.

Principle

Real-time light. The interface responds to time of day.

VOLTR application

Page theme shifts with hour — cool blue before dawn, warm gold at sunset, deep indigo at night — driven by Date().getHours().

Car Launch Event Lighting

Porsche Taycan Launch

Porsche Taycan world premiere

Technique

Single rotating follow-spot on car, black cyc wall, haze.

Principle

Theatrical reveal: dark → light → detail.

VOLTR application

Page load starts black, fades to hero in 1.2s with a single moving highlight that traces diagonally across content.

Rimac Nevera Reveal

Rimac Nevera debut

Technique

Pixel-mapped LED floor + absolute darkness above.

Principle

Ground plane defines space. Above is void.

VOLTR application

Navigation items hover with a "ground glow" — light pool beneath each word, not above. Inverting expectation.

EQXX Light Box Room

Mercedes-Benz EQXX debut

Technique

Light box room — white walls, white floor, car lit from all sides evenly.

Principle

Total diffusion. No shadows. Pure objecthood.

VOLTR application

Minimal UI mode: no card shadows, no gradients, just crisp content on uniformly lit surface — engineering precision.

F-150 Lightning Launch

Ford F-150 Lightning debut

Technique

Blue LED light channels in floor converging on vehicle.

Principle

Directional light paths guide the eye.

Scroll indicators as animated light bands that travel along path lines toward the next section.

Diffuse warmth Arrival, EQXX Soft gradients over hard edges. Feathered over sharp.
