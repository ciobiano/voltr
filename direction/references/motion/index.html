Mechanical Motion References

Automotive Gauge Needle Sweep

Power-on animation (Mercedes, Audi, BMW)

Needle sweeps 0 → redline → back-to-idle in 1.2s with easeInOutCubic. The "self-test" is the brand mark in motion.

VOLTR pattern: Logo entry animation: mark sweeps in from -90° rotation to 0°, then "settles" with a 3° overshoot bounce back. Exactly like a tachometer. Use gauge sweep easing (0.42, 0, 0.58, 1).

Porsche PCM UI Knob Detents

Porsche PCM infotainment

Rotation snaps to positions with 50ms magnetic catch. Haptic-esque UI without haptics — felt through sight.

VOLTR pattern: Button clicks have micro-"dent" animation: scale to 0.95 over 60ms, snap back over 150ms. Simulates mechanical switch.

Tesla Yoke UI — Card Arrival

Tesla Model S yoke UI

Cards slide in with 200ms travel, stop with 30ms settle oscillation. Quick arrival, fast settle, no float.

VOLTR pattern: Side-panel entries: 200ms slide, 30ms rubber-band settle. No easing-out — they arrive firmly.

BMW iDrive Rotary Controller

BMW iDrive

Menu scrolling has physical "notch" resistance every 24px. Discrete steps with tactile illusion.

VOLTR pattern: Scroll snapping with acceleration blur: between snap points the content blurs 2px, clears on stop — simulates mechanical detent.

Apple Watch Crown Scroll

watchOS

Acceleration maps rotation speed to scroll distance non-linearly. Faster spin = more distance per tick.

VOLTR pattern: Mouse wheel multiplier: at slow scroll, 1:1 mapping. At fast flick, 1:4. Curve: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1).

Automotive Commercial Camera Movements

Mercedes "Chasing the Light" (2024)

20-second single take: crane boom from interior dial detail → pulls up through sunroof → reveals mountain road. Camera moves are narrative.

VOLTR pattern: Page transitions "boom up" from detail to context. A micro-infographic scales down and retreats upward as the next section's hero enters from below.

Audi "The Next Chapter"

Slow dolly push-in through a forest of reflective columns; car emerges at end. Push-in = focus, commitment.

VOLTR pattern: Scroll-driven push-in: as user scrolls, the camera pushes into a data visualization, which becomes background for the next section.

Porsche "The Weight of Light"

360° orbit on crane around car at 0.5x speed. Slowest orbit signals importance.

VOLTR pattern: Hero product on landing page rotates 3° per scroll tick — a full 360° takes 120 scroll events. The slowest rotation on the page.

Ronin 4D "Smooth Drive" Mode

Algorithmic acceleration ramp: 0→peak over 1.5s, peak→0 over 2s. Slow-in: 40% of duration, fast middle: 20%, slow-out: 40%.

VOLTR pattern: This is where (0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) comes from. Used for all scroll-triggered reveals.

Camera Stabilization Principles

Steadicam Float

The Shining hallway, gimbal operation

Drifting float with micro-corrections. Never perfectly still. Imperceptible movement = tension.

VOLTR pattern: Page has a 0.1° perpetual tilt-rotate that resets on user interaction — "alive" without being distracting.

ARRI Trinity — Ground-Level Glide

Low-mode shots glide inches above surface. Proximity = groundedness.

VOLTR pattern: Sticky headers move at 0.8x scroll speed with 1.2s catch-up spring — feels like heavy camera on tracks.

Per-Axis Mass

MoVI Pro "ramp" profile

Each axis has different mass. X-movements (horizontal reveals) ease differently from Y (scrolling).

VOLTR pattern: Horizontal reveals are more damped than vertical scroll. Different easing for different axes.

System Extraction — Motion Principles

Principle Rule
No bounce No spring easing, no playful overshoot. Every motion has weight.
Gimbal easing Primary curve (0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) for all section reveals. 40% slow-in, 20% fast middle, 40% slow-out.
Duration hierarchy micro: 60-100ms, ui: 180-400ms, scene: 800-1400ms, camera: 2000-4000ms
Mechanical metaphor All micro-interactions simulate physical detents and switches
Fade-through-black Primary section transition: 300ms out, 200ms black hold, 300ms in
Match-cut shapes Every section transition shares one geometric element
Scroll speed = mass Heavy elements move slower than light ones. 0.8x for headers, 0.5x for backgrounds.
