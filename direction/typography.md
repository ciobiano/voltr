# typography.md

# VOLTR Typography System

# Core Philosophy

Typography is atmosphere.

Text should not feel promotional.
It should feel cinematic, architectural, and emotionally controlled.

VOLTR typography communicates:

* precision
* restraint
* clarity
* intelligence
* calm confidence

---

# Typography Direction

VOLTR combines:

* Swiss typography systems
* architectural editorial spacing
* cinematic pacing

The typography should feel engineered rather than expressive.

---

# Typography Personality

The system should feel:

* clean
* spacious
* structured
* mathematically balanced
* highly readable

Avoid:

* futuristic gimmick fonts
* aggressive condensed typography
* excessive decorative styling
* loud marketing hierarchy

---

# Font Direction

## Primary Typeface

Recommended characteristics:

* neutral sans-serif
* geometric restraint
* strong readability
* refined spacing

Examples:

* Helvetica Now
* Suisse Intl
* Inter Tight
* General Sans

---

## Secondary Typeface

Used for:

* specifications
* telemetry
* technical metrics
* engineering labels

Examples:

* IBM Plex Mono
* Geist Mono

---

# Hierarchy System

## Display Text

Purpose:
Hero statements and cinematic headlines.

Characteristics:

* large scale
* wide tracking
* medium-light weight
* strong negative space

Should feel monumental without aggression.

---

## Heading Text

Purpose:
Section framing and directional hierarchy.

Characteristics:

* uppercase restraint
* balanced spacing
* structured rhythm

---

## Body Text

Purpose:
Narrative explanation and supporting information.

Characteristics:

* restrained line length
* calm readability
* minimal visual noise

---

## Metric Typography

Purpose:
Performance stats and engineering information.

Characteristics:

* technical precision
* stable alignment
* monospaced consistency

---

# Typography Rhythm

VOLTR typography should breathe.

Use:

* shorter statements
* deliberate pacing
* larger spacing
* visual silence

Text density should remain low.

---

# Headline Philosophy

Headlines should feel:

* emotionally restrained
* cinematic
* intentional
* intelligent

Avoid:

* startup hype language
* exaggerated futurism
* loud marketing copy

---

# Good Examples

Motion without noise.

Technology through invisibility.

Engineered for silence.

Precision in motion.

---

# Bad Examples

THE FUTURE OF TRANSPORTATION

REVOLUTIONARY AI PERFORMANCE

THE NEXT GENERATION EXPERIENCE

---

# Spacing Rules

Typography spacing should feel architectural.

Prefer:

* generous line height
* controlled letter spacing
* large section spacing
* stable alignment systems

Avoid cramped typography.

---

# Alignment Principles

VOLTR primarily favors:

* left alignment
* grounded compositions
* structured grid systems

Centered layouts should be used sparingly for emotional emphasis only.

---

# Responsive Philosophy

Typography should scale fluidly across devices.

Large typography should maintain:

* elegance
* readability
* negative space
* pacing

Even on smaller screens.

---

# Motion + Typography

Typography transitions should feel cinematic.

Preferred behavior:

* masked reveals
* fade emergence
* subtle tracking transitions
* spatial continuity

Avoid:

* bouncing text
* excessive stagger chaos
* flashy typography motion

---

# Final Principle

VOLTR typography should feel like:

architectural language suspended in motion.

Calm.
Precise.
Controlled.
Timeless.
